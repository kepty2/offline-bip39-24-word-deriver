Step 04 · Wallet settings

Derive public wallet information.

This is a separate deliberate step. It converts the current mnemonic into a BIP39 seed, derives the hardened BIP84 account, retains only its public account node, and then clears temporary secret byte arrays on a best-effort basis.

Passphrase warning: a different passphrase creates a completely different wallet, even with the same 24 words. A forgotten or mistyped passphrase cannot be recovered.

Step 07 · Verification checklist

Match before funding.

  1. Create the mnemonic and optional passphrase on the permanently offline device.
  2. Back up the mnemonic and passphrase physically.
  3. Generate the public watch-only package.
  4. Transfer the ZPUB or descriptors to the online watch-only wallet using QR.
  5. Confirm that the first several receiving addresses shown online exactly match this offline application.
  6. Do not send bitcoin until the addresses match.
  7. After verification, use the online watch-only wallet for future receiving addresses.
  8. Keep the seed phrase and passphrase permanently offline.
